Hushrise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Hushrise is an alarm app. Alarm settings, including times, repeat days, labels, sounds and preferences, are stored on your device. The app uses Apple's AlarmKit to schedule alarms you create.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time purchase. Hushrise uses Apple StoreKit to check purchase status and restore access. Apple handles payment information; Hushrise does not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics and diagnostics

Hushrise has no developer-operated account, advertising or analytics service. The app may write limited technical information to the device system log to help diagnose errors. Apple provides its own operating system and App Store services under its privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

You can delete alarms within the app. Removing the app removes its local app data; App Store purchase records are managed by Apple.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact futs0420@163.com or visit Hushrise Support.

Changes

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated.
