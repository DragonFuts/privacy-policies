WearPick Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

WearPick is an outfit journal for iPhone. This policy explains the information the app handles and the Apple services it uses. The developer is 海龙 江.

Your outfits and wear history

WearPick stores the outfit photos, look names, occasions, temperature ranges, ratings, optional clothing items and notes that you add. It also stores wear dates, weather snapshots attached to wear records, plans, recommendation preferences, achievement progress and technical records needed to save and merge your changes.

The system photo picker provides the images you select; the app does not browse your full photo library. Camera access is optional. Imported photos are prepared as JPEG images for storage.

Local storage and backups

Your library and photos are stored in the app's local storage. Exported WearPick backups contain library information and available photos. Anyone who can access a backup file may be able to read its contents, so store and share it carefully. Importing a backup merges content with the current library.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync in the app, WearPick uses the private CloudKit database for your signed-in Apple Account to sync library changes and photos. This may include saved looks, wear records, plans, clothing items, notes, preferences and achievement information. Technical synchronization records associate changes with the selected account and cloud environment. Sync pauses when an account change requires confirmation.

Apple operates CloudKit under its own terms and privacy practices. The app does not operate a separate developer cloud server or add its own end-to-end encryption layer before CloudKit upload. Sync availability depends on account status, connectivity, storage and service availability. Keep a backup of important content.

Location and weather

With your permission, WearPick requests a location while you use the app and sends coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to obtain local weather. The app requests kilometer-level location accuracy. You can deny location access and enter a temperature range manually.

The app caches weather on the device using an installation/account partition, coarse region and time zone. Automatic forecast caches are excluded from device backup and from the app's library backup and CloudKit library sync. A weather snapshot saved with a confirmed wear, or manually entered temperature information, may remain in the library and be included in optional library sync or exported backups. Exact coordinates are not stored with your outfit records.

Purchases

When available, WearPick Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. WearPick uses verified App Store transactions to determine access to Pro features and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple manages payment information and App Store purchase history.

Advertising, tracking and diagnostics

WearPick does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not use advertising identifiers or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Technical errors may be written to the device's system log using an operation context, error type, domain and code. There is no developer-operated log collection service in the app.

Retention and deletion

Content stays in the library until you remove it or remove the app's data. Removing a look or wear from the interface does not necessarily erase every underlying photo or history entry immediately: the library retains change history and deletion markers for merging and synchronization. Copies in exports, device backups, other devices or iCloud may remain after local changes or uninstalling the app. Disabling sync does not itself erase existing cloud copies. Contact us for questions about deletion; do not send private outfit photos unless necessary.
