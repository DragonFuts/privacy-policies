For help with WearPick, email futs0420@163.com.

Getting started

Add an outfit photo in Looks, then save a name and comfortable temperature range. Open Today to choose a saved look. Confirming a look records a wear; browsing and drawing candidates do not.

Weather

Location access is optional. If local weather is unavailable, enter today's temperature range manually.

Your calendar

Calendar combines plans and wear history. Up to three wears can be confirmed for a local day. Weekly plan saving requires Pro.

Backups and iCloud

My contains backup export/import and optional iCloud controls. Keep exported backups somewhere private. An import merges with the current library. Sync requires the same Apple Account, connectivity and available iCloud storage; it is not a substitute for checking that important content has been saved.

WearPick Pro

When available, Pro is a one-time App Store purchase. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If the product is unavailable, do not attempt repeated purchases; contact support.

Contacting support

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Avoid sending private photos, backups or Apple Account credentials.
