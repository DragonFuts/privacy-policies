DearYou Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

DearYou is a private writing app. This policy describes information handled by the app and by Apple services you choose to use with it.

Information you add

DearYou stores the private passcode verifier and the information you choose to enter: people’s nicknames and optional photos, whisper text, mood labels, and photos attached to whispers. The app does not send your whispers to their subjects or provide a reply feature.

Selected images are imported through Apple’s system photo picker. DearYou receives only the photos you select; it does not browse your photo library. Images are resized before they are saved.

On-device storage and protection

Your private space is stored in the app’s protected storage. The database and photos are encrypted on this device and protected with iOS file protection. The app does not store your passcode as readable text. Face ID is optional; iOS performs biometric matching, and DearYou does not receive your face data.

DearYou excludes its private-space files from ordinary device backup. If you do not turn on iCloud sync, your private space stays on this device and is not available for cloud recovery.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, DearYou syncs private-space records and selected photos to the private CloudKit database for the Apple Account signed in on your device. Apple operates CloudKit and processes this data under Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy practices. DearYou does not operate a cloud server and does not add a separate end-to-end encryption layer to the data before CloudKit upload. Apple’s standard iCloud data protection applies; Advanced Data Protection is controlled separately in your Apple Account settings.

Only content confirmed as fully synced can be restored from iCloud using the same Apple Account. If sync is unavailable or iCloud storage is full, new or incomplete content remains only on this device and is not included in a cloud restore.

Purchases

DearYou Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. DearYou receives purchase verification needed to unlock the feature, but does not receive your payment-card details. Purchase history and payment information are handled by Apple.

Diagnostics, advertising, and analytics

DearYou does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send private-space content to the developer. The app may write limited technical diagnostics, such as an operation name and error code, to the device’s system log. These diagnostics do not include whisper text, photos, or your passcode. The app has no developer-operated service that collects these logs.

Retention and deletion

Your content remains in the app until you delete it. In Privacy settings, you can clear this device, or clear this device and the synced iCloud copy. Clearing the iCloud copy does not erase copies already stored on your other devices. App Store purchase history is managed by Apple and is not removed when you clear your DearYou content.

Changes to this policy
