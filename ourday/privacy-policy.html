Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026

Ourday helps adults share household tasks, income and expenses, calendar events, and subscription records. This policy describes the app’s data flows. It does not describe Apple’s separate operation of iCloud or Apple Health.

Family records in iCloud

When you create or join a family, Ourday uses Apple CloudKit to store the family name, tasks, ledger entries, events, subscriptions, and related membership and change information. This includes titles and notes you enter, amounts and currencies, subscription billing cycles, expected payment dates and billing time zones, subscription status, categories, dates, assignees, payers, event participants, reminder choices, record identifiers, creator/editor identifiers, timestamps, deletion status, change history and operation receipts used to check save results and prevent duplicate writes.

The family creator owns the family’s CloudKit records in their iCloud space. Invited members use their own iCloud accounts to access the shared records. Members with accepted access can edit shared business records. Sharing is private, not open to anyone who has the link. An iCloud email entered for an invitation is sent to Apple to identify the intended participant; CloudKit membership can expose participant names and identifiers to the family.

The app requires online requests to view and change shared records. It does not provide an offline browsing database or a background upload queue. A draft being edited may remain temporarily on the device while an error or uncertain save is resolved. Apple processes iCloud data under its own service terms and privacy practices; Ourday does not operate a separate household-data server.

Personal health stays outside family sharing

Only when you choose to connect a health category does Ourday request read access through HealthKit. Depending on your choices and available records, it reads activity, workouts, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, weight, BMI and existing blood-pressure measurements, with sample dates, units and source information.

Ourday uses these readings on your device to display personal summaries and trends. It does not write health records, send the readings to the family’s CloudKit records, include them in family exports or notifications, or share them with other family members. Apple Health and Apple’s own health-sync settings operate separately. Missing readable data does not tell the app whether permission was refused.

Ourday is not a medical service and does not provide diagnosis, medical advice, abnormality alerts or real-time health monitoring.

Notifications and local preferences

Language and reminder preferences are stored locally using the app’s preferences storage. If you enable notifications, the device schedules reminders for tasks assigned to you, events you participate in, shared subscriptions, and an optional daily count of plans. Subscription reminders are available to each accepted family member on their own authorized, successfully synced device; disabling a subscription reminder changes the shared setting, while your device’s notification permission remains personal. A reminder may show a task, event or subscription title and date on the lock screen according to your iOS settings; subscription reminders do not include the recorded amount or note. Updates made on another device are reflected after this device refreshes; timely delivery and immediate remote cancellation are not guaranteed. These are local notifications, not a separate developer-operated messaging service.

Advertising, accounts and payments

The current app has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK, bank connection, in-app purchase or separate Ourday account. Subscription records are entered manually: Ourday does not retrieve your Apple or bank subscriptions, make payments, cancel a service with its provider, or treat an expected payment as a confirmed charge. iCloud authentication is handled by Apple; the app does not request your Apple Account password. Apple or TestFlight may process service or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. The app uses local system error logging without intentionally writing household content or health values to those logs.

Retention, leaving and deletion

Leaving a family or being removed revokes your shared access; it does not delete the family’s existing records, including records you created. A normal delete moves a business record to the family trash, where it remains until restored, permanently removed by the creator, or the family is dissolved. There is no automatic trash-expiry timer in this version. Change-history and operation metadata can remain after the underlying business record is permanently removed; this is not a tamper-proof audit service.

The creator can prepare a JSON export of family records, membership references and history, excluding personal health. The app creates a temporary file and requests its removal after the sharing screen is closed. Anyone you send or save an export to controls that separate copy. Avoid sharing it with unintended recipients.

After an explicit confirmation, dissolving a family requests deletion of its CloudKit record zone and removes shared access. This does not erase copies already exported by others, Apple Health records, or guarantee immediate erasure of every Apple-managed backup or service record. A failed or uncertain cloud request must be checked before assuming deletion has completed. Uninstalling the app is not a request to dissolve a family or delete iCloud records.

Your controls and questions

Use Ourday Settings to change language and reminders and open family management, history or trash where available. Manage notification and Health permissions in iOS Settings or Health. Only the family creator can remove members or dissolve the family. There is no separate Ourday account to delete; deleting an Apple Account is managed by Apple.

For questions about this policy, email Ourday support. Do not include health records, invitation links, exports or Apple Account credentials in support requests.

Getting started and family invitations

Create a family on your own or accept a private invitation while using the intended recipient’s iCloud account. Shared tasks, spending, events and subscriptions require an internet connection. A pending invitation does not grant access; check the iCloud account and invitation status with the family creator.

Saving, health and reminders

If a save result is unconfirmed, check the original operation before creating the same entry again. For conflicts, review the latest record before retrying. Health readings depend on your permissions and recording sources; manage access in Apple Health or iOS Settings. Other devices’ changes affect local reminders after this device refreshes.

Send a useful report

Include your app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, app language and steps to reproduce the problem. Redact personal names, titles and amounts from screenshots. Do not send passwords, health records, family exports or invitation links.
