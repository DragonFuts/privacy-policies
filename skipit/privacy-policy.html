Skipit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Skipit handles information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Skipit stores game history, scores, personal bests, Daily Challenge attempts, and app preferences on your device. Skipit does not require an account. These records are not sent to a Skipit-operated server or synced by Skipit between devices. Apple device backups may include app data according to your backup settings.

You can remove local game records from Settings by choosing “Clear All Local Game Data.” Deleting the app may also remove data stored in its app container. Data retained in a device backup is managed through your Apple device and account settings.

Photos

If you choose “Save Monument,” Skipit may request permission to add the generated image to Photos. This permission is limited to adding an image: Skipit does not read or browse your existing photo library. Photos you have already saved are not removed when you clear Skipit's local game data.

Purchases

Skipit Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. Skipit receives the purchase entitlement needed to unlock features; it does not receive or store your payment-card details. Clearing local game data does not remove an App Store purchase. Purchases can be restored with the Apple Account used to make them.

Network services, advertising, and analytics

Skipit does not operate an account or game-data service and does not include advertising or gameplay analytics SDKs. App Store purchase and restore operations use Apple's services. Skipit does not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

If Skipit's data practices change, this policy will be updated on this page.
