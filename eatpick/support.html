Getting started

Add foods in Foods, each with a photo. Open Today to get a pick, then choose it or try another. Choosing a pick does not add a meal; use Log this meal to record it in History.

History and tags

History shows your recorded meals in a calendar or list. Tags and suitable meal types help you filter what Today suggests.

iCloud

iCloud sync is optional and is found in Settings. Sync requires the same Apple Account, connectivity and available iCloud storage; it is not a substitute for checking that important content has been saved.

EatPick Pro

Pro is a one-time App Store purchase with no subscription. It unlocks weekly and monthly reviews, saved filters and meal scopes. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If the price does not load, try again later or contact support.

Contacting support

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Avoid sending private photos or Apple Account credentials.
