EatPick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

EatPick: Meal Journal is a food and meal journal for iPhone. This policy explains the information the app handles and the Apple services it uses. The developer is 海龙 江.

What you add to the app

EatPick stores the foods, photos, names, tags, suitable meal types, preferences, meal records, dates and notes that you add. It also stores your recommendation settings, such as saved filters and meal scopes if you use Pro, and technical records needed to save and merge your changes.

The system photo picker provides the images you select; the app does not browse your full photo library. Camera access is optional, and you can choose a photo from your library instead. Photos are prepared and stored with your library.

Local storage

Your library and photos are stored in the app's local storage on your device. The app also keeps small preferences on the device, such as your layout and sort choices and whether you dismissed a weekly summary card.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync in the app, EatPick uses the private CloudKit database for your signed-in Apple Account to sync your library and photos between your devices. Technical synchronization records associate changes with the selected account. Sync pauses when an account change requires confirmation.

Apple operates CloudKit under its own terms and privacy practices. The developer cannot access your private CloudKit data, and the app does not operate a separate developer server. Sync availability depends on account status, connectivity and iCloud storage. Keep your own backups of important content.

Purchases

EatPick Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. EatPick uses verified App Store transactions to determine access to Pro features and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple manages payment information and App Store purchase history.

Data collection, advertising and tracking

The developer does not collect your data. EatPick does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. The app does not request your location.

Retention and deletion

Content stays in the library until you remove it or remove the app's data. Removing a food or meal in the app does not necessarily erase every underlying record immediately, because the library retains change history and deletion markers needed for merging and synchronization. Copies in device backups, other devices or iCloud may remain after local changes or uninstalling the app. Disabling sync does not itself erase existing cloud copies. Contact us with questions about deletion; do not send private photos unless necessary.
