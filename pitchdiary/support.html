Common questions

How does sync work? Sign in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account. PitchDiary syncs through your private iCloud database; check status in More > Settings.

Can I back up my data? Yes. Use More > Settings > Export to save a JSON backup, and Import to restore or merge it.
