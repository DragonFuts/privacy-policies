PitchDiary Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PitchDiary is a personal soccer diary. The matches, boots, self-assessment ratings, captions and settings you enter are stored on your device. PitchDiary has no developer-operated account or server, and the developer cannot see your data.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, PitchDiary uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your records to the private database in your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is not accessible to the developer. If iCloud is unavailable, PitchDiary keeps working with data on your device.

Photos

If you allow access, PitchDiary links match photos and videos from your photo library by reference so you can view them with your matches. Originals are not copied or uploaded by PitchDiary. Share cards you choose to save are added to your photo library. You can change photo access at any time in iPhone Settings.

Export and sharing

Backups (JSON) and match lists (CSV) are created only when you export them, and go where you choose through the system share sheet. Share cards are images you create and share yourself.

Analytics and diagnostics

PitchDiary has no advertising, tracking or analytics service. The app may write limited technical information, such as error types, to the device system log to help diagnose problems; your records are not included.

Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete records in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Data synced to iCloud can be managed in your iCloud settings.

Children

PitchDiary is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact futs0420@163.com or visit PitchDiary Support.

Changes

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated.
