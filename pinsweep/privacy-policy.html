PinSweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

PinSweep helps you review and manage photos in your Apple Photos library. This policy explains what information PinSweep accesses and how it is handled.

Photos

PinSweep accesses only the static photos that you authorize through Apple Photos. Photo content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to PinSweep servers. Limited Photo Access is respected: photos outside your selected authorization scope are not shown, counted, or deleted by PinSweep.

Local Pins and Settings

Your Pin and Pending decisions, preferences, and other app state are stored locally on your device. PinSweep does not send this information to a server. Local Pins may be removed when you uninstall the app or clear its local data.

Photo Deletion

When you confirm a deletion, PinSweep asks Apple Photos to move the selected photos to Recently Deleted. Apple Photos controls the system confirmation and retention of those photos.

Purchases

PinSweep Pro purchases and restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit and the App Store. PinSweep receives the entitlement result needed to unlock Pro features. PinSweep does not receive or store your payment details.

Tracking and Data Sharing

PinSweep does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs. PinSweep does not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new update date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue in the PinSweep project repository.
